With a x1.5 design margin and a 85% converter, that is **4.6 mA from the cell**. One recording keeps the device awake about 60 s, so it costs **0.084 mAh** including two haptic pulses.

Standby (radio off, AFE rail off, self-discharge included): **0.14 mAh/day**.

| 402025 | 150 | 4.0 x 20 x 26.5 | 252 days | 23 days |

Days between charges, using 80 % of the cell. A 10-second recording is so short that the spec's 150-300 mAh is far more than the device needs; an 80-100 mAh cell already gives a shared clinic device more than a week.
